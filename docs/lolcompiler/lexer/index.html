Lexical analyzer (Task 1).

The lexer reads the source one character at a time and follows the state machine from my Phase 1 design:

  • START: skip whitespace. A # moves to the ANNOTATION state, any other character moves to the TEXT state.
  • ANNOTATION: keep adding letters until whitespace, another #, or the end of the file. If the word is #MAEK or #GIMMEH, go to the KEYWORD state to read the word that must follow it.
  • KEYWORD: skip spaces, then read the next word (HEAD, PARAGRAF, …) and glue it on, so the token becomes e.g. #MAEK HEAD.
  • TEXT: keep adding characters until a # or the end of the file.

Annotations are case-insensitive, so they are stored in upper case. Plain text keeps its original case.

Why #MAEK HEAD is one token: in the BNF, #MAEK starts a head, a paragraph and a list, and #GIMMEH starts a title, bold, item, link and variable use. With only one token of lookahead the parser could not choose between them. Joining the two words fixes that and keeps the grammar LL(1).
